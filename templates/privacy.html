Privacy Policy & Academic Disclosure

Last updated: Academic Year 2026 • Major Project Submission

ACADEMIC RESEARCH PROTOTYPE DISCLAIMER: Blue Line is developed as a University B.Tech Major Project prototype. In Demo Mode, emergency contact dispatches and police notifications are simulated to demonstrate software architecture and algorithms without incurring commercial SMS/telephony fees or transmitting false distress signals to civil emergency dispatchers.
1. Geolocation Data Processing

Your precise device location (latitude, longitude, and accuracy) is acquired through the W3C Geolocation API only when an emergency event is triggered (or when explicitly testing the location feature). Real-time breadcrumbs are logged during an active incident and GPS tracking stops immediately once the user marks the emergency as "Resolved" or "False Alarm".

2. Sensor & Motion Information

Accelerometer readings via DeviceMotionEvent are processed entirely on the client-side device. Sensor data is never streamed to external servers or logged persistently unless a sudden impact threshold is crossed and confirmed by the user.

3. Voice Emergency Keyword Detection

Voice monitoring uses the browser's native Web Speech API solely to listen for the configured distress trigger word (such as "HELP"). No audio files are recorded, uploaded, or stored on our servers.

4. Police Station Information & Distance Matching

Nearest police station calculation is performed using the mathematical Haversine great-circle distance algorithm against verified records stored in our database. The application does NOT transmit automated calls to real police stations without external authorized government API integrations.

5. Responsible Emergency Use

In real life-threatening circumstances, users must always prioritize dialing national emergency helplines directly: 112 (National Emergency Support), 100 (Police), or 108 (Ambulance).
