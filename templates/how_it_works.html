Architectural Workflow

How Blue Line Protects You

A 9-step fail-safe workflow engineered to verify threats, prevent false alarms, and dispatch emergency response coordinates immediately.

1
User Registration

Create a secure profile with phone number, home address, and blood group. Passwords are encrypted using Werkzeug PBKDF2 hashing.

2
Add Emergency Contacts

Store verified family and friends with phone numbers and relationships (Father, Mother, Friend) and priority order.

3
Enable Protection

Toggle physical impact detection (accelerometer) or hands-free distress keyword monitoring ("HELP") right from your dashboard.

4
Emergency Detected

Triggered either manually via the large SOS button, by physical shock (>25 m/s²), or spoken distress keywords.

5
10-Second Countdown

A critical safety window allows the user to tap "I am Safe - Cancel" to prevent accidental triggers and avoid false alarm panic.

6
GPS Location Resolution

HTML5 Geolocation API resolves current latitude, longitude, and accuracy radius, generating a dynamic Google Maps URL.

7
Contacts Notified

The notification engine transmits structured incident details, live navigation links, and incident nature to all active contacts.

8
Live Tracking & Police Lookup

Continuous GPS streaming updates the Leaflet map while the Haversine formula identifies the closest registered police station.

9
Incident Resolution

Once safe, the user taps "Resolve Emergency". GPS watchPosition is cleared and the complete trajectory is preserved in database history.
